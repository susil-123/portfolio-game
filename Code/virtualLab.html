Overview

This project is a fully interactive Virtual Mechanical Engineering Lab designed to solve a real problem that emerged during COVID—students were unable to access hands-on machinery and practical workshops. I built this environment so B.Tech Mechanical Engineering students could experience practical training remotely, with accurate, functional simulations of real industrial machines.

The lab features a fully operable Turn Master 35 Lathe and a KUKA industrial robot arm, both recreated with realistic controls and behavior so students can practice machining and automation tasks exactly as they would in a physical workshop.

A built-in Lab Assistant Companion guides students through different modules, helping them navigate the lab and understand each machine’s purpose.

Every lab also includes an AI Lab Master, implemented using CONV.AI and Metahumans. These AI specialists are trained on machine-specific datasets and can answer questions, clarify concepts, explain procedures, and provide real-time support—replicating the experience of talking to a knowledgeable instructor on the shop floor.

This project delivers a complete remote-learning solution that gives mechanical engineering students:

  • Hands-on machine operation experience

  • Instructor-like guidance through AI lab masters

  • An immersive, interactive learning environment

All without needing physical access to industrial machines.
