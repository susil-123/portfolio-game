Overview

This system implements a custom flanking behavior on top of A* pathfinding, ensuring enemies never run directly at the player. Instead, they approach in a deliberate criss-cross (zig-zag) pattern while continuously correcting their path to maintain visibility and stay on valid navigation routes.

Technical Details


The AI dynamically alternates lateral offsets as it advances, creating a predictable-but-hard-to-hit flanking motion. To prevent the pattern from pulling the enemy off-course, the system automatically:

  • Adjusts the criss-cross offset when walls or obstacles would break the pattern.

  • Maintains a forward “B-line” component, ensuring consistent pressure toward the player.

  • Keeps the player within line of sight throughout movement, recalculating offsets when visibility is lost.

  • Integrates with A* so the zig-zag motion always remains on a valid, walkable path.


The result is an enemy that feels intentionally evasive, aggressive, and tactical—approaching the player from shifting angles instead of running straight forward or getting stuck behind geometry.
