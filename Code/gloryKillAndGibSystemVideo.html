Overview

The Glory Kill & Gib System provides a high-impact finisher mechanic inspired by DOOM Eternal. Enemies have per-limb damage, a stagger state, and cinematic synced executions where both player and enemy rigs animate together.

1. Per-Limb Damage

Each enemy has separate hitboxes and HP for Head, Torso, Arms, Legs.

  • Weak point multipliers enable strategic combat.

  • Limb damage modifies AI behavior (limping, reduced attacks).

  • Certain limbs can be destroyed, affecting available glory kill animations.

2. Stagger & Execution State

Enemies enter a Stagger State when health drops below a threshold or a weak point is destroyed.

Staggered enemies:

  • Play stagger animations

  • Stop attacking

  • The player can trigger a Glory Kill when within a defined range and facing the target.

3. Synced Player–Enemy Execution Animations

On activation, both characters:

  • Lock movement

  • Snap to aligned execution positions

  • Play synced root-motion animations

Animation chosen based on:

  • Approach direction (front/back/side)

  • Limb condition

  • Enemy type

Camera and VFX sync via animation notifies.

After the kill, the enemy transitions to ragdoll or gib state or handmade animation depending on the enemy/ kill effect the system supports them all.

4. Rig Switching & Mesh Tearing

Enemies switch from their base rig to a Glory Kill rig containing:

  • Pre-sliced meshes

  • Gore layers or mechanical internals

Used for kills involving:

  • Limb tearing

  • Head ripping

  • Body splitting

Mesh parts detach and convert to physics objects if required.
