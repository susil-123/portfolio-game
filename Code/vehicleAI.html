Overview

A fully custom vehicle AI system designed to simulate believable convoy behavior, adaptive driving, and smart recovery—built without relying on marketplace assets.

Technical Details

Rubber-Banding Convoy System


The AI vehicles dynamically follow a primary convoy leader using a robust spot-request and registration system.

  • Adaptive Following: Each vehicle requests an available formation spot and accelerates or decelerates depending on its distance from the target position.

  • Smart Speed Control: Vehicles automatically slow down when approaching their assigned point or when detecting upcoming turns.

  • Contextual Handling: Traction and steering automatically adjust based on turn intensity, creating smoother and more realistic maneuvering.


Advanced Vehicle Pathfinding & Recovery


The AI is engineered to detect and correct driving failures, mimicking real-life vehicle behavior in constrained environments.

  • Crash Detection & Self-Correction:When a vehicle crashes or becomes misaligned, it intelligently recalibrates and resumes its path.

  • Narrow-Space Recovery: If stuck in a tight corner, the vehicle performs realistic reverse-and-forward “wiggle” maneuvers—similar to freeing a real car from a tight parking spot.

  • Dynamic Turning Radius: The AI adjusts its turning behavior in real time. During extremely tight turns, it widens its path and even creates a controlled, drift-like motion to maintain flow and speed.
