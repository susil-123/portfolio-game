About This Project

This vertical slice represents a solo-developed FPS that combines the momentum-rich traversal of Titanfall 2 with the relentless, aggressive combat flow of DOOM Eternal. Players can chain together advanced movement techniques — slide, slide-hop, jump, ground slam, wall run, and grapple hook — to maintain high-speed flow and outmaneuver cyborg-zombie enemies.

Combat is built around a Quick Swap system that rewards switching weapons between shots for maximum burst damage and combo potential. The slice features two handcrafted levels, two unique weapons, and a full set of custom enemies.

Every system, animation, and asset is completely original — no marketplace content used.

Proudly, this project was selected for the IGDC 2025 Indie Top 40 Showcase, recognizing it as one of the standout indie titles of the year.
