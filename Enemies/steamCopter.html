SteamCopter — LOS-Reactive Aerial Skirmisher

SteamCopter is a light aerial enemy kept aloft by dual rotor assemblies mounted on its arms. It specializes in evasive mid-air behavior and reacts dynamically to player attention.

Core Behavior

  • Hovers at mid-range using two independently driven steam rotors.

  • Continuously repositions to maintain distance and harassment angles.

  • If the player maintains line of sight for too long, SteamCopter performs a rapid evasive dash, breaking LOS and forcing the player to reacquire the target.

Rotor Weak-Point System

Each arm contains a rotor that can be individually damaged:

One Rotor Destroyed

  • Loses ability to perform evasive dashes.

  • Hover stability becomes uneven, reducing mobility and making it easier to hit.

Both Rotors Destroyed

  • Loses all lift.

  • Crashes uncontrollably into the ground.

  • Detonates on impact, dealing AoE damage to enemies (and potentially the player if too close).

Design Intent

SteamCopter creates:

  • A reactive aerial threat that punishes tunnel vision,

  • A weak-point driven interaction where disabling mobility is as important as dealing damage,

  • Dynamic aerial silhouettes and movement variety within combat spaces.
