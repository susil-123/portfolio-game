FlamePouch — Bullet-Hell Fuel-Core Spider

FlamePouch is a multi-torso, spider-like ranged enemy designed to overwhelm players with high-volume projectile spam. Its four arms and dual torsos enable rapid firing, creating localized bullet-hell patterns.

Core Behavior

  • Slow, deliberate movement to maintain constant pressure.

  • Continuously launches multiple projectiles in staggered volleys.

  • Attack density scales with proximity and line of sight.

Fuel Pouch Mechanic (Core Weak Point)

Equipped with a flammable fuel pouch that powers its projectile attacks.

  • The pouch can be heated by shooting it directly.

  • Upon exceeding a heat threshold, the pouch:

    • Explodes, dealing heavy damage to FlamePouch.

    • Weakens all its projectiles, reducing their speed and damage.

  • The pouch naturally cools down over time, forcing the player to:

    • Maintain pressure and repeatedly heat it,

    • Or risk the enemy returning to full attack capability.

Design Intent

FlamePouch introduces:

  • Sustained bullet-hell pressure,

  • A controlled weak-point gameplay loop,

  • A risk-reward interaction where players must expose themselves to fire while overheating the fuel core.

The result is a high-threat enemy that rewards offensive play.
