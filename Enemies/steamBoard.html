SteamBoard — High-Mobility Flanker on Hover Skates

SteamBoard is a fast, evasive enemy built around aggressive flanking movement. Riding a steam-powered hover board, he executes continuous criss-cross traversal patterns to stay unpredictable while closing the distance to the player.

Core Behavior

  • Uses a custom criss-cross movement algorithm to constantly shift lateral direction, preventing easy tracking.

  • Tries to flank rather than approach in a straight line, adjusting angles dynamically based on player position and visibility.

  • When within attack radius, he performs a high-speed dash directly at the player.

Dash Attack

The front of the hover board contains dual needle-like blades.

  • A successful dash collision deals heavy piercing damage.

  • Dash timing and angle are chosen based on both player movement and SteamBoard’s lateral momentum.

Hover Board Weak Point

The board itself can be damaged independently.

  • Reducing the board’s health:

    • Lowers his max speed

    • Breaks his criss-cross rhythm

    • Makes his dash attacks easier to dodge and telegraph

  • Fully disabling the board leaves him significantly slower and vulnerable.

Design Intent

SteamBoard is designed to:

  • Challenge player tracking skills,

  • Add pressure through fast flanking movement,

  • Provide a tactical weak-point system that rewards disabling mobility.
