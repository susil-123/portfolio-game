Dead Head — Adaptive Heat-Scaling Artillery Unit

Dead Head is a mid-range artillery enemy built around a heat-scaling combat mechanic. A nozzle mounted on its skull vents energy as its internal temperature rises, visually communicating threat level and gameplay state.

Core Behavior

Continuously charges heat over time using a float-curve–driven scaling system.

As heat increases:

  • Damage ramps up

  • Movement speed increases

  • Projectile (cannonball) speed increases

  • Attack cooldown decreases

All combat parameters scale dynamically, making the enemy progressively more dangerous the longer it survives.

Attack Pattern

Fires high-impact cannonballs from the head nozzle.

  • Projectile arcs and speed adjust automatically based on current heat level.

  • Designed to pressure players into prioritizing the target before it becomes overwhelming.

Weak Point & Reset Mechanic

If the player shoots and destroys the exposed head, the enemy enters a bleed-out state:

  • Heat and all scaling curves reset to baseline.

  • Bleeding causes heavy visual feedback and damage vulnerability.

  • Provides a strategic window for the player to finish the kill.

Design Intent

Dead Head forces players to:

  • Stay mobile to avoid increasingly fast artillery shots.

  • Prioritize weak-point targeting to prevent full heat escalation.

  • Use the bleed-out window to maximize DPS and control battlefield pacing.

The result is an enemy that rewards precision.
