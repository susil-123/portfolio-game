Overview

I developed a fully procedural foot placement system for spider-like creatures using Unreal Engine’s Control Rig and Full Body IK.

The setup uses per-leg cycle offsets, world-space foot locking, predictive sphere traces, and a dynamic velocity-based stride system.

Each foot alternates between locked (stance) and unlocked (swing) states, and a predictive trace adjusts the next foot location based on movement direction and speed. The result is natural stepping, even on uneven or moving surfaces.

Finally, all updated foot transforms feed into UE’s Full Body IK solver, allowing the body and other legs to automatically stabilize around procedural leg motion.
