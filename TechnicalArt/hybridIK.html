Overview

This system blends handcrafted enemy animations with procedural IK to create responsive, target-aware behavior without sacrificing animation quality. The base locomotion, attack, and idle cycles are authored traditionally, while layered IK modifies only what needs to be adaptive—mirroring techniques discussed in the DOOM GDC talk.

Target-Aware Upper Body IK

  • Preserves the original animation’s body movement, timing, and style.

  • Smooth blending ensures natural transitions without snapping.

  • Allows enemies to track the player even during complex animations (running, turning, attacking).

Foot Locking & Ground IK

  • Per-foot IK traces the ground and adjusts foot position and rotation.

  • Foot locking prevents sliding during locomotion cycles and turning-in-place animations.

  • The system blends with baked animations, correcting only the lower body where necessary.

  • IK activates adaptively based on movement speed and animation phase.

Hybrid Animation Pipeline

  • Traditional animations define personality, timing, and weight.

  • IK layers correct alignment, maintain grounding, and add responsiveness.

  • Runtime constraints ensure adjustments never break silhouette or animation intent.

  • All modifications happen additively, so animators’ work remains intact.

Result

A highly reactive, polished enemy animation system where characters:

  • Track the player naturally

  • Maintain stable foot placement on uneven terrain

  • Retain the artistic quality of the original animations

  • Feel grounded, weighty, and responsive—similar to DOOM’s hybrid IK approach
